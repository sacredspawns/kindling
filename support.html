Frequently Asked

The fire went out and my run ended. Did I lose all my progress?

Only the current run ends. Tinder you earned and unlocks (cards, archetypes, Forge/Hearth upgrades) are permanent and carry over to your next run.

How does the save system work?

Your run auto-saves at the start of every in-game day. Close the app any time and reopen later — you'll land back on the same dawn. There is no manual save, no save slots, and no cloud save.

Can I play offline?

Yes. The game makes no network requests at any point. There is no online mode, no leaderboard server, and no account.

Are there in-app purchases or ads?

No. Kindling is a one-time purchase. There are no ads, no IAP, no premium currency, and no subscriptions.

I want a refund.

Refunds are handled by the platform you bought it through:

  • App Store: reportaproblem.apple.com
  • Google Play: Open Google Play → Menu → Account → Order history → Request refund

We can't process refunds directly.

The game crashes or freezes on my device.

Email us at support@sacredspawns.com with your device model, OS version, and what you were doing when it happened. A screen recording, if you have one, is gold.

How do I delete my data?

Kindling stores everything (saves, settings, local analytics log) inside the app's private storage on your device. Uninstall the app, or use your device's per-app storage clear option, to erase all data. There is nothing to delete on any server because nothing is sent to one.

Can I stream or record Kindling on YouTube/Twitch?

Yes, including monetized channels. No permission needed. If you'd like us to amplify a video, send the link to support@sacredspawns.com.

I have an idea for a card / boss / scenario.

Send it our way. We can't promise to use every suggestion, but we read them and they shape what we build next.

System Requirements

  • iOS: iPhone running iOS 14 or later. Portrait orientation. ~80 MB install.
  • Android: Android 7.0 (Nougat) or later. Portrait orientation. ~85 MB install.
  • Network: Not required.

Known Issues

Live bug list is maintained internally; if you hit something that looks broken (UI clipping, frozen run, audio dropouts), email us and we'll log it and respond.
