The Game

The cold came early this year. You have a handful of cards, a fire that must not go out, and thirty days to survive. Each dawn you draw a hand of action cards — gather, hunt, build, trap, rest, forge. Every choice shifts your fire, your warmth, your wood, your food, your health. Spend poorly and the night will take you.

Every five days a boss attacks. Wolves, raiders, frost bears, the Wendigo, the Winter King and beyond. Forge weapons, craft armor, build structures, earn perks to stand a chance.
