Privacy Policy

Kindling: Last Fire — Sacred Spawns

Short version: Kindling does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. Everything stays on your device.

What We Collect

Nothing that leaves your device. Kindling stores the following data locally only:

  • Save data — your current run progress, stored in the app's private storage.
  • Settings — preferences like mute state, saved locally.
  • Play analytics — a local log of gameplay events (cards played, runs completed, days survived). This file lives only on your device and is never sent anywhere.

What We Do Not Collect

  • No name, email address, or account information
  • No location data
  • No device identifiers
  • No usage data sent to any server
  • No crash reports transmitted externally

Third-Party Services

Kindling contains no third-party SDKs, advertising networks, or analytics services. There are no in-app purchases, accounts, or social features.

The game is distributed through the Google Play Store and Apple App Store. Those platforms have their own privacy policies governing data they collect independently of this app.

Network Access

Kindling makes no network requests. There is no server, no telemetry endpoint, and no cloud sync. The game works entirely offline.

Children

Kindling does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13. Because no data is collected at all, the game is safe for all ages from a privacy standpoint.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new effective date.
